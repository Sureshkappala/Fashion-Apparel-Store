Textile Provenance

Noble Natural Fibres

We source exclusively from historic family mills across Italy, France, and Scotland.

Biella, Italy

Virgin Merino Wool

Milled by 4th generation weavers using sub-alpine spring water. Unmatched thermoregulation, natural crease recovery, and signature drape.

Como, Italy

22-Momme Mulberry Silk

Spun on heritage shuttle looms on the banks of Lake Como. Lustrous liquid sheen, hypoallergenic softness, and featherlight breathability.

Normandy, France

Organic Long-Staple Flax

Dew-retted coastal flax cultivated without synthetic chemicals. Naturally antibacterial, cooling, and grows softer with every wear.

Tuscany, Italy

Vegetable-Tanned Leather

Tanned using chestnut and mimosa bark tannins over 40 days. Free from toxic heavy metals and develops a rich, individual patina over decades.

Artisan Method

The 4-Step Sartorial Journey

Every tailored garment undergoes a meticulous, unhurried creation cycle.

01

Silhouette Study

Personal measurement profile capturing 28 distinct biometric angles to engineer an exact anatomical fit.

02

Muslin Prototype

Hand-drafted canvas prototype built to test stance, posture balance, and natural movement before cutting the final cloth.

03

Hand-Canvasing

Over 1,200 hand stitches secure the floating horsehair chest canvas, allowing the jacket to mould perfectly to your chest.

04

Archival Handover

Final steam press, horn button attachment, hand-embroidered provenance tag, and delivery in breathable cotton garment cases.
