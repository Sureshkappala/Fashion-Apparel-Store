Client Assistance

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Details regarding shipping, custom tailoring, fabric care, and returns.

Each garment page features an exact measurement chart in both centimeters and inches. Additionally, our stylists offer complimentary virtual sizing checks. If you fall between sizes, our tailoring team recommends ordering the larger size for structured coats or smaller for bias-cut silk.

We ship via express carbon-neutral couriers. European orders arrive in 1-2 business days; North America and Asia Pacific arrive within 2-4 business days. All duties and customs are fully prepaid by STACKLY at checkout.

We recommend eco-friendly green dry cleaning for structured coats and blazers once per season. Silk slips can be gently hand-washed in cool water with specialized silk detergent and dried flat in the shade. Each order arrives with a breathable archival garment bag.
